Frequently asked questions

What is brow architecture and shaping?

Nikolai sets the shape to your facial proportions and corrects it by removing stray hairs, so the brows look neat and natural.

What is brow lamination?

A treatment that sets the brow hairs in the direction you want: they look fuller, neater and brushed-up for weeks, with no daily styling.

And lash lamination?

It lifts and curls your natural lashes from the root, with no extensions: your eyes look more open and the lashes look longer.
