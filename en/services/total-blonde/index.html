Frequently asked questions

How much is a total blonde?

From ARS 228,000 to ARS 568,000, depending on length, density and your starting colour. A total blonde touch-up costs ARS 250,000.

Which products do you use?

Schwarzkopf Professional BLONDME — KULTURA is an official partner of the brand. We look after the quality of your hair at every stage.

How long does it take?

Up to five hours the first time and about three and a half hours for a touch-up.
