Frequently asked questions

What is toning?

It’s a gentle colour that refreshes your shade: it neutralises yellow or orange, adds shine and leaves the colour clean. At KULTURA we tone with gel colour.

How much is it?

Single-tone colour or toning ranges from ARS 128,000 to ARS 224,000 depending on length. Root colour costs ARS 114,000.

How often should I tone?

When the shade starts to look dull or yellowish. At home, a toning shampoo such as BLONDME Purple helps the result last.
