A Russian beauty salon in Buenos Aires

Russia is known for its service and its approach to beauty. Here’s what we brought to Argentina.

A school with its own name

In Russia beauty is part of everyday culture: clients expect precision, neatness and results that last. Those standards produced techniques now known worldwide by the country’s name, such as the Russian manicure.

KULTURA is a Russian beauty studio in Microcentro, Buenos Aires. We brought that way of working to Argentina: attention to detail, careful technique and premium service, with an international standard of quality.

What you’ll notice in the result

  • Russian manicure. With an e-file the cuticle is removed completely and the polish sits right against the skin. Hands stay neat for longer.
  • Russian SMART pedicure. Foot care with SMART discs, with or without foot treatment and gel polish.
  • Colour. Techniques like AirTouch, which lighten only the strands that need it and protect the hair at the touch-up.
  • Cuts. Layers that take out weight and add movement without losing length, for women and men.
  • Lashes and brows. Extensions with hypoallergenic materials, brow architecture, and brow and lash lamination.

How we work

Every service starts with listening: what you want, how you live with your hair or nails, how much upkeep you’re up for. We book the time the work needs, with no rush. KULTURA is an official Schwarzkopf Professional partner, and you can buy home care in the studio.

Languages

Our masters speak Spanish and Russian. If you speak English, we’ll look after you with the help of a translator.

Where we are

Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro, Buenos Aires. Open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm. Book online or message us on WhatsApp.
