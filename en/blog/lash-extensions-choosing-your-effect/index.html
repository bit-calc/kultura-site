Lash extensions: choosing your effect

Doll, fox, squirrel or anime: how the effects differ and how to choose yours.

Extensions make your eyes more expressive without mascara: synthetic lashes or fine fans are attached one by one to your natural lashes. Length, curl and density are chosen to suit the shape of your eyes, so the same effect looks different on every face.

Classic effects

  • Doll: the longest lashes sit in the centre of the lid. The eye looks more open and round.
  • Fox: length builds towards the outer corner. The look is elongated and more graphic.
  • Squirrel: the longest lashes sit just before the outer corner, then get shorter again at the very end. It visually lifts the eye.

Current effects

The anime effect creates spikes: longer fans gathered into fine points over the base row, like anime characters. Sofía also does trend and American effects, and helps you choose the one that suits you best.

Materials

Sofía has tested a huge range of materials from different countries and kept the best: hypoallergenic and suited to her clients. A wide choice of curls makes it possible to hit exactly the effect you want.

About Sofía

She has been doing lash extensions for four years. She started in Russia and, after moving to Argentina, worked in several studios and salons. She has completed many training and advanced courses, backed by numerous certificates.

Aftercare

  • For the first day, keep them dry.
  • Don’t rub your eyes or sleep face down on the pillow.
  • Oil-free make-up removers: oil weakens the adhesive.
  • Brush them with a spoolie every day.
  • Refills every two to four weeks; Sofía will tell you when.

Training

Sofía also trains beginners, giving the basic knowledge for anyone who wants to learn the profession.
