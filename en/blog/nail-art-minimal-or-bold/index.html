Minimal or bold?

At KULTURA you don’t have to choose just one: from nude to stamping and decals.

We listen to your ideas and turn them into a manicure that reflects your style. Here’s what we can do on top of a Russian manicure.

Stamping

A pattern is transferred to the nail from a metal plate with a special stamp. The result is fine, identical designs (lace, animal print, geometry) on every nail in a few minutes.

Decals and stickers

Thin films with a printed design. They go on over the colour and are sealed with top coat, so the edges don’t catch and the design lasts as long as the polish. Great for complex graphics, lettering or tiny details.

And more

  • Hand-painted: from dots to hearts and lettering.
  • Gradient: one colour fading softly into another.
  • Chrome and powders: a mirror or pearl shine.
  • Foil for metallic accents.
  • Matte top instead of gloss.

What it costs

Nail art on all nails adds from ARS 17,500 to the manicure. Bring a reference or just tell us what you like, and we’ll suggest how to do it.
