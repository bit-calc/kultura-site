AirTouch in Buenos Aires: prices, where to get it and answers to common questions

What people ask most before lightening their hair: what AirTouch is, how it differs from balayage, what it costs and where to get it in Buenos Aires.

AirTouch is one of the most requested lightening techniques for a natural-looking blonde with no hard line at the roots. This guide brings together the questions we hear most: first the concrete ones — where, how much and with whom — and then the general ones, so you can choose with confidence even if you haven’t settled on a technique yet.

What is AirTouch?

It is a lightening technique in which each section is separated with the air of a hairdryer: the shorter, finer hairs are blown out and stay unlightened, and only the hair that remains is worked on. The result is a diffused blonde with soft transitions and roots that grow out without a visible line.

How is AirTouch different from balayage and highlights?

In balayage the lightener is painted freehand onto the surface of the section: it gives a sun-lightened effect, with more contrast between mid-lengths and ends. Classic highlights are woven from the root and wrapped in foil: the lightening is more even and the regrowth line shows sooner. AirTouch sits between the two: the sections are fine and lightened close to the root, but the air leaves natural hair between them, so regrowth is far less noticeable.

Who is AirTouch for?

Anyone who wants a natural-looking blonde or lightening and doesn’t want to touch up the roots every month. It works on light and brown hair; on dark or previously coloured hair the lightening is done gradually, sometimes over more than one session. If the goal is an even blonde from roots to ends, that is a different service: total blonde.

Does AirTouch damage hair?

Any lightening changes the hair fibre; what changes the outcome is how much product is used, on what hair and how carefully. In an AirTouch touch-up, Roman does not re-apply product to hair that is already lightened: he works on the regrowth only. To see how your hair responds before a full session, there is the strand test.

How often does AirTouch need a touch-up?

Because the root is diffused, the lightening is usually touched up every four to six months. The tone — cooler, more beige, more golden — washes out sooner: a toning service between sessions brings it back.

How do you look after blonde hair at home?

  • Shampoo and conditioner for coloured hair. They cleanse without stripping the tone.
  • Purple shampoo once a week. It neutralises the yellow that appears with washing.
  • Heat protection before the dryer or straightener. Heat dulls colour faster than washing does.
  • A mask once or twice a week. Lightened hair needs more moisture.

How do you choose where to get AirTouch?

  • Look at the colourist’s real work, especially on a base similar to yours: lightening natural brown hair and hair dyed dark are different jobs.
  • Ask for a consultation and a strand test. They show which shade is possible and in how many sessions.
  • Ask for the final price before you start. With AirTouch it depends on length and density.
  • Ask how long the session takes and keep the day free: it is several hours of work.
