Russian manicure in Buenos Aires: prices and what’s included

What each nail service costs at KULTURA, how long it takes and what changes the price.

A Russian manicure takes longer than a regular one: it’s done dry, with an e-file, and the polish is applied right up to the cuticle. These are KULTURA’s prices in Microcentro, as of November 2026.

Manicure prices

  • Russian manicure with strengthening — from ARS 64,500. E-file manicure, nail strengthening and gel polish. Up to two and a half hours.
  • Manicure, no polish — from ARS 42,500. The e-file manicure without colour. One hour.
  • Gel extensions — from ARS 92,000. Sculpted gel nails in the length and shape you choose. Up to three and a half hours.
  • Extension refill — from ARS 55,000. For sculpted nails that have grown out. Up to two and a half hours.
  • Removal, no new polish — from ARS 23,000. The product comes off and the nail is left clean. Up to one hour.
  • Nail art on all nails — from ARS 17,500. Added to the manicure.

What changes the price

  • Strengthening. The Russian manicure with strengthening includes a layer that levels the nail and makes it tougher; the colour goes on top.
  • Length. If you want longer nails it becomes gel extensions, which take more time and product.
  • Nail art. French, stamping, decals, ombré, chrome or hand-painted designs are added to the manicure.
  • Removal. If you only want the product off, with no new polish, that’s a separate service.

If you already have a design in mind, send us the photo on WhatsApp to ask about the price.

Hands and feet in one visit

The four-hand manicure and pedicure combines the Russian manicure with strengthening and the full Russian SMART pedicure: two artists work at once and it all takes about two and a half hours. It costs ARS 142,000.

How often you’ll come back

Polish from a Russian manicure usually lasts three to four weeks, and because it sits right up to the cuticle the regrowth takes a while to show. That’s the usual interval between visits.

Who does the manicure

Valeria and Tatiana, KULTURA’s manicure and pedicure artists. The studio works by appointment, Monday to Saturday, 10 to 20, at Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a Russian manicure at KULTURA?

With strengthening and polish, from ARS 64,500; without polish, from ARS 42,500 (November 2026).

How long does a Russian manicure take?

With strengthening and polish, up to two and a half hours; without polish, one hour.

How much are sculpted nails?

Gel extensions cost from ARS 92,000 and a refill from ARS 55,000.

Is nail art included in the price?

No: nail art on all nails is added to the manicure and costs from ARS 17,500.
