French: the classic that never gets old

A white line along the tip, and dozens of variations around it.

A classic French is a nude or pink base with a fine white line along the free edge of the nail. It works for the office and for a wedding, and keeps hands polished without anything extra.

Variations

  • Classic: a white line on a nude base.
  • Colour: the line in any shade, from neon to chocolate.
  • Micro French: a very fine line, great on short nails.
  • Reverse: the accent at the base of the nail instead of the tip.
  • Double line or a French with details.

Shape changes everything

On almond or oval nails a French looks softer; on square nails, more graphic. The line follows the shape of your nails so it matches on all ten fingers.

How long it lasts

On gel polish over a Russian manicure, a French lasts as long as a plain colour: usually three to four weeks. The price depends on the variation; nail art on all nails starts from ARS 17,500.
