How to remove gel polish without damaging your nails

Why you shouldn’t peel it off, how it’s removed in the salon and what to do if it lifts early.

Gel polish is made to last for weeks, which is why it doesn’t come off like regular polish. Often what weakens the nail isn’t the product but the way it’s taken off.

Why you shouldn’t peel it off

The gel is bonded to the top layer of the nail. When you lift or peel it, part of that layer comes away with it: the nail is left thin, rough and quick to break. Filing it off by hand without experience carries the same risk: it’s easy to go too far and reach the nail.

How it’s removed in the salon

In a Russian manicure the product is removed with an e-file: the technician takes it down layer by layer with the right bit and stops before the natural nail. Then the cuticle is done and the nail is ready for new polish or to be left bare.

If it lifts early

  • Don’t pull at the edge. Even if part of it is loose, the rest is still bonded to the nail.
  • Don’t cover it with another polish. Moisture collects under the lifted product.
  • Book a removal or a new manicure. It’s the safest option for the nail.

If you want to go bare

With careful removal the nail stays intact. If you’d like to go without colour for a while, you can book removal with no new polish.
