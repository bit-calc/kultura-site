Hair salon in Microcentro: cuts, colour and blow-dry near the office

What we do for hair, what it costs and how to book at KULTURA.

KULTURA is a Russian beauty studio right in Microcentro, Buenos Aires: Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683. If you work or run errands in the area, you can have a cut, colour or blow-dry done without crossing the city.

What we do for hair

  • Women’s and men’s cuts. Women’s cut ARS 73,000, men’s cut ARS 48,000, fringe trim ARS 24,500. Cut and treatment, ARS 114,000.
  • Single-tone colour, roots and toning. Single-tone colour or toning from ARS 128,000 to ARS 224,000; root colour, ARS 114,000.
  • AirTouch, balayage and highlights. From ARS 180,000 to ARS 455,000. A strand test can be done first, ARS 20,000.
  • Total blonde. From ARS 228,000 to ARS 568,000.
  • Blow-dry and wash. Heat styling ARS 97,000; wash ARS 24,000.
  • Hair treatments. Treatment ARS 81,500; together with colouring, ARS 48,000.
  • Grey blending and colour removal. The price is set at the consultation.

Who does your hair

Hair is done by Roman, co-founder and head colourist at KULTURA. We work with Schwarzkopf Professional: the studio is an official partner of the brand and its products can be bought on site.

Hours and booking

We are open Monday to Saturday, 10 to 20, by appointment only. Book online or message us on WhatsApp and we will send the available times.

In your language

We speak Spanish and Russian. If you speak English, we will help you with a translator.

How to get here

Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro, C1038AAM, Buenos Aires. At the bottom of this page there is a “Get directions” link with the map.

Frequently asked questions

Where is there a hair salon in Microcentro, Buenos Aires?

KULTURA is at Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro, and does cuts, colour, AirTouch, total blonde, blow-dries and hair treatments.

How much is a haircut at KULTURA?

Women’s cut ARS 73,000, men’s cut ARS 48,000 and fringe trim ARS 24,500 (November 2026).

Do you take walk-ins?

No, by appointment only, Monday to Saturday from 10 to 20.
