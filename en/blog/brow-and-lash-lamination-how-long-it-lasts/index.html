Brow and lash lamination: what it is and how long it lasts

Two treatments that tidy what you already have, with no daily make-up. Plus brow architecture.

Brow lamination

A treatment that softens the brow hairs and sets them in the direction you choose, usually upwards. Brows look fuller, neater and brushed-up, with no gel needed each morning. Great if your brows have unruly hairs, grow downwards or you want the brushed-up look.

How long it lasts

Usually four to eight weeks: the effect goes as the hairs naturally renew. It depends on your hair type and aftercare.

Lash lamination

Lifts and curls your natural lashes from the root, with no extensions. Your eyes look more open and the lashes look longer. A good alternative if you don’t want to maintain extensions.

Brow architecture and shaping

Before lamination it’s worth setting the shape. In brow architecture the shape is designed to your facial proportions and corrected by removing stray hairs. Both can be done in the same appointment.

Aftercare for the first 24 hours

  • Keep the area dry, and skip steam, sauna and the pool.
  • No make-up or rubbing on the brows or lashes.
  • Afterwards, brush your brows each morning with a spoolie and, if needed, a nourishing oil your master recommends.

When to wait

If your skin is irritated, you have any injury in the area or a known allergy, say so beforehand — your master will tell you whether to wait. Laminating too often can dry the hairs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does brow lamination last?

Usually four to eight weeks, depending on your hair and aftercare.

Are there downsides?

Doing it too often can dry the hairs, and it’s not advised on irritated skin.

Can it be combined with shaping?

Yes: the shape comes first, then the lamination, in one appointment.
