Hair, nails, lashes and brows in one place: your beauty day in Microcentro

Come once and leave with your colour, cut, hands, feet and lashes done.

KULTURA is a Russian beauty salon in Microcentro, Buenos Aires, where hair, nails, lashes and brows are done at one address: Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683. There is no need to spread your week across a hairdresser, a nail salon and a lash studio: pick a day, come once and leave with everything done.

What you can have done in one visit

  • AirTouch, balayage and highlights. From ARS 180,000 to ARS 455,000; a session takes up to six hours.
  • Total blonde. From ARS 228,000 to ARS 568,000; about five hours.
  • Single-tone colour, toning and roots. Colour from ARS 128,000 to ARS 224,000, about two and a half hours; root colour ARS 114,000, two hours.
  • Women’s, men’s and children’s cuts. Women’s cut ARS 73,000, an hour and a half; men’s cut ARS 48,000, an hour; fringe trim ARS 24,500. Ask for the price of a children’s cut on WhatsApp.
  • Blow-dry and styling. With a wash, ARS 97,000; an hour and a half.
  • Hair treatment. ARS 81,500, an hour; added to a colour, ARS 48,000.
  • Russian manicure. With strengthening ARS 64,500, up to two and a half hours; without polish ARS 42,500, an hour.
  • Russian SMART pedicure. Full pedicure with foot care and polish, ARS 77,500; two and a half hours.
  • Four-hand manicure and pedicure. Two nail artists work at the same time: hands and feet done in about two and a half hours.
  • Lash extensions. With Sofía: classic and trend effects.
  • Brows and lamination. Brow shaping, brow lamination and lash lamination with Nikolai. Ask for the price on WhatsApp.

How to plan your beauty day

Message us on WhatsApp with what you would like done and we will suggest the order and the times, one service after another so you are not left waiting. Times are confirmed when you book, depending on each specialist’s schedule.

  • Hands and feet in one trip. Four-hand manicure and pedicure: about two and a half hours.
  • Cut and nails. Women’s cut and Russian manicure with strengthening: about four hours.
  • Before an event. Blow-dry with a wash plus four-hand manicure and pedicure: about four hours.
  • Roots, cut and nails. Root colour, women’s cut and Russian manicure with strengthening: about six hours.
  • A full change of look. AirTouch and Russian manicure with strengthening: up to eight and a half hours, the whole day.

These are guide times with the services one after another.

Who it suits

People who work downtown and want everything sorted in one outing. People who travel in from far away and prefer one trip to three. Anyone getting ready for a wedding, a party or a trip who wants hair, nails and lashes on the same day. And anyone coming with company: we cut hair for women, men and children.

Who looks after you

Hair is done by Roman, co-founder and head colourist at KULTURA. Nails are done by Valeria and Tatiana, lashes by Sofía and brows by Nikolai. We work with Schwarzkopf Professional: the studio is an official partner of the brand.

Hours, booking and language

We are open Monday to Saturday, 10 to 20, by appointment only. We speak Spanish and Russian; in English we help you with a translator.

How to get here

Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro, C1038AAM, Buenos Aires. At the bottom of this page there is a “Get directions” link with the map.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a salon in Buenos Aires that does hair and nails in one place?

Yes. KULTURA, at Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683 in Microcentro, does colour, cuts, blow-dries, hair treatments, Russian manicure, Russian SMART pedicure, lash extensions and brows.

Can I have everything done on the same day?

Yes, by appointment. Tell us on WhatsApp which services you want and we will line them up according to the schedule. As a guide: a cut and a manicure take about four hours; an AirTouch, up to six.

How long do a manicure and a pedicure take together?

With four hands, about two and a half hours: two nail artists work at the same time.

Do you cut children’s hair?

Yes, we cut children’s hair too. Ask for the price on WhatsApp.

Do you take walk-ins?

No, by appointment only, Monday to Saturday from 10 to 20.
