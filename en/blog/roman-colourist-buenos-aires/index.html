Roman, head colourist at KULTURA: techniques, prices and booking

Who does the colour and cuts at KULTURA, which techniques he works with, what his services cost and how to book with him.

Roman is co-founder of KULTURA Beauty Studio and the studio’s head colourist, in Microcentro, Buenos Aires. He is our specialist in healthy, beautiful hair: he does the colour, cuts, treatments and styling.

Roman, in a few lines

  • Role. Co-founder of KULTURA and the studio’s head colourist.
  • His work includes. AirTouch, contouring, dimensional colour, gel toning, colour removal and layered cuts.
  • Where. KULTURA Beauty Studio, Tte. Gral. Juan D. Perón 683, Microcentro, Buenos Aires. Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 20:00, by appointment.
  • Languages. The studio works in Spanish and Russian; English with a translator.
  • Instagram. @roma_pokrasit

Which techniques does Roman work with?

  • AirTouch. Before lightening, the hair is separated with air from the dryer and only the strongest strands are worked. The transitions are soft and the regrowth more natural, on a dark base too.
  • Total blonde. With Schwarzkopf Professional BLONDME and care for the hair at every stage.
  • Contouring. The colour is worked into the strands that frame the face: light around the face.
  • Dimensional colour and grey blending. Depth and contrast with a natural result; grey is blended with the hair colour, without a hard regrowth line.
  • Toning and single-tone colour. Clean shades with gel colour; root colour too.
  • Colour removal. To return to a natural, even shade without damaging the hair.
  • Layered cuts. They take out excess weight and add movement and volume without losing length. He cuts women’s and men’s hair.

How much do Roman’s services cost?

  • AirTouch, balayage or highlights: from ARS 180,000 to ARS 455,000, depending on length and density. Up to six hours.
  • Total blonde: from ARS 228,000 to ARS 568,000; a touch-up from ARS 250,000.
  • Contouring: from ARS 170,000.
  • Single-tone colour or toning: from ARS 128,000 to ARS 224,000; root colour from ARS 114,000.
  • Women’s cut: from ARS 73,000. Men’s cut: from ARS 48,000. Cut and treatment: from ARS 114,000.
  • Hair treatment: from ARS 81,500. Heat styling: from ARS 97,000.
  • Strand test: ARS 20,000, about one hour.

Grey blending and colour removal are priced at the consultation. Prices as of November 2026.

Which products does he work with?

KULTURA is an official Schwarzkopf Professional partner. Home-care products are available in the studio.
